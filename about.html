FASHION.

UrbanThread started in 2024 with one screen, one dryer and a stubborn rule: if it can't survive 30 washes, it doesn't ship.

From a Shahpur Jat courtyard

We're a two-person studio that got tired of 150 GSM "premium" tees that die in a month. So we built the opposite: heavyweight blanks sourced by GSM, printed by hand, numbered by run, and sold only in small drops.

Drop 002 is live now — eight pieces, 120 units each. When they're gone, we start drop 003, and nothing from 002 gets reprinted. That's the whole scarcity story. No artificial hype, just math.

Everything is made within 6 km of the studio. The fabric comes from Panipat mills, the printing happens in the courtyard, and the fitting happens on you.
